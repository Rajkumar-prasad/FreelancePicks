# FreelancePicks content plan

Goal: rank for long-tail "cost", "checklist" and "how to" searches from people launching online stores, and send that traffic to the 8 service pages (where the affiliate buttons are) and the free Store Launch Kit.

## Research findings (November 2026)

- Head terms like "hire Shopify developer" or "logo designer" are dominated by Fiverr, Upwork, agencies and big publishers. A new domain will not rank there for a long time.
- Cost questions ("how much does X cost") have strong buyer intent and are winnable with specific, sourced numbers and clear tables.
- Checklists are crowded but mostly thin listicles. A detailed, practical checklist tied to the free kit can stand out.
- How-to questions about safe access and tracking (collaborator access, partner access, Pixel testing) have little good content and show real expertise.
- Comparison pages ("Fiverr vs Upwork") are high intent. Fiverr ranks for its own comparison, so ours must be balanced and focused on store owners, with a clear affiliate disclosure.
- No keyword volume tool was available for this round. Priorities are based on intent, SERP competition and fit with the service pages. Validate with Google Search Console data after 6 to 8 weeks.

## Rules for every article

- Research first, then outline, then write. Use real, sourced numbers and list sources at the bottom.
- Answer the question in the first 2 to 3 sentences.
- Link to at least 2 service pages and 1 related guide. Link on descriptive anchor text, never "click here".
- Regular hyphens, no em dashes.
- No fake reviews, fake case studies or invented personal stories. Opinions and tips are fine.
- Update prices every 6 months and refresh the "Updated" date.

- 2 to 3 articles per week. At that pace the plan is done in about 3 months.

- Month 2: start link building (guest posts on ecommerce and marketing blogs, your own sites and profiles).
